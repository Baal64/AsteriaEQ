# ADR-0005 - Use of MCP23017 GPIO Expander

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-08-05
- **Decider:** Alexandre Ba

---

## Context

The Asteria EQ hardware platform is based on an ATmega32U4
(Pro Micro).

The project requires a growing number of digital inputs and outputs:

- STEP outputs;
- DIR outputs;
- SPI Chip Selects;
- ST-4 guiding inputs;
- joystick inputs;
- status LEDs;
- driver ENABLE signals;
- future extensions.

The available GPIOs on the Pro Micro are limited.

Furthermore, not all signals have the same timing constraints.

Some signals require deterministic hardware timing, while others only change
occasionally.

---

## Decision

The project uses one MCP23017 16-bit GPIO expander connected through the
hardware I²C bus.

The MCP23017 is dedicated to low-frequency digital inputs and outputs.

Time-critical signals remain connected directly to the ATmega32U4.

The initial allocation is:

| MCP23017 Pin | Function |
|--------------|----------|
| GPA0 | RA ENABLE |
| GPA1 | DEC ENABLE |
| GPA2 | Status LED |
| GPA3 | Joystick push button |
| GPA4-GPA7 | Reserved |
| GPB0-GPB7 | Reserved |

---

## Consequences

### Positive

- Sixteen additional GPIOs.
- Minimal MCU pin consumption.
- Excellent scalability.
- Clear separation between fast and slow signals.
- Simplified future hardware evolution.

### Negative

- One additional component.
- I²C communication latency.
- Additional firmware driver required.

---

## Alternatives Considered

### Direct connection to the Pro Micro

Every signal connected directly to the MCU.

**Advantages**

- Simpler hardware.
- No I²C dependency.

#### **Disadvantages**

- Very limited GPIO availability.
- Poor scalability.
- Difficult future expansion.

Rejected.

---

### PCF8574

An 8-bit GPIO expander was initially considered.

#### **Advantages**

- Lower cost.
- Simple interface.

#### **Disadvantages**

- Half the available GPIOs.
- Less flexibility.
- Rapidly reaches its limits.

Rejected.

---

## Impact on Architecture

The Core layer remains completely independent of the GPIO implementation.

The firmware manipulates abstract interfaces such as:

- `IDigitalOutput`
- `IDigitalInput`

The Platform layer provides the concrete implementations:

- `Atmega32u4DigitalOutput`
- `Mcp23017DigitalOutput`

Both satisfy the same interfaces.

No modification of the Core layer is required when moving a signal from the
MCU to the MCP23017.

## Notes

The MCP23017 is not intended for deterministic real-time signals.

It must never be used for:

- STEP generation;
- SPI communication;
- high-frequency timing.

Its purpose is to host low-frequency GPIOs and future hardware extensions.

---

## Design Principle

Time-critical signals stay on the MCU.

Low-frequency signals may be delegated to the GPIO expander.

This keeps the firmware scalable while preserving deterministic behaviour.
